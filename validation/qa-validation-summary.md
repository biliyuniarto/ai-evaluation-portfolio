# Quality Validation Summary

This page summarizes de-identified QA feedback from completed AI evaluation work.

## What the reviews consistently validated

Across multiple reviewed submissions, the strongest recurring signals were:

- ratings that were internally consistent with the written rationale
- preferences supported by observable differences between the two model responses
- clear, specific comparative reasoning rather than generic preference statements
- fair treatment of both models, including appropriate tie decisions where performance was comparable
- correct separation of dimensions such as utility, audio quality, conversational dynamics, and overall preference
- no unsupported error flags in the reviewed examples shown here

## Selected QA evidence

One reviewed submission received:

- **Final rating: 5/5**
- **Computed score: 100%**
- **Rubric issues: 0/8**
- **Overall result: Exceptional**

The audit marked all listed passing criteria as passed, including scenario adherence, scenario coherence, rating-dimension quality, error-cluster identification, and rating-justification quality.

Other reviewed submissions were also rated **Exceptional**, with feedback noting that the evaluation was consistent, supported by relevant evidence, and that the final preference was reasonably justified by the observed model differences.

## What this demonstrates

The reviews support the same evaluation approach used throughout this portfolio:

1. Observe both responses independently.
2. Compare them on equivalent dimensions.
3. Separate absolute task success from relative preference.
4. Avoid inventing differences when both models perform similarly.
5. Support the final judgment with specific, verifiable evidence.
